# 🎬 My Favorite Series: Stranger Things

**Overview:** *Stranger Things* is a sci-fi horror drama created by **The Duffer Brothers** that premiered in 2016. ~~It was originally titled Montauk.~~

---

## 🌟 Why It's My Favorite
- **Great Storyline:** A group of kids uncovers secret government experiments and supernatural forces in their small town.
- **Memorable Characters:** Strong character development, especially for *Eleven* and *Steve Harrington*.
- **80s Nostalgia:** Amazing retro soundtrack and cinematography.

## 🏆 Top 3 Episodes
1. **Season 4, Episode 7** – *The Massacre at Hawkins Lab*
2. **Season 4, Episode 9** – *The Piggyback*
3. **Season 3, Episode 8** – *The Battle of Starcourt*
